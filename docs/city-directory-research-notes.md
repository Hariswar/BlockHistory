The guide gives the St. Louis Room telephone as 314-539-0371 and says materials can be scanned or photocopied. It does not establish which Haines years are held; confirm the specific editions with staff.

These are holdings records, not digitized address entries. Confirm whether each edition has a street/address section and where staff can retrieve it. Select **St. Louis city**, rather than the separate St. Louis County directory.

## How to complete each pending record

For each target and each requested year, append one record using this format. Photograph or scan the directory title/year page and the entire relevant address entry with its page number. Preserve multiple occupants as separate lines under the same record.

```text
Target ID:
Requested year: 1960 / 1970 / 1980
Actual edition year, including any two-year span:
Directory title and publisher, exactly as printed:
Library / department / call number or microfilm reel:
Section and printed page number (or frame if unpaginated):
Street heading and address exactly as printed:
Business/occupant exactly as printed:
Business type exactly as printed, if given:
Verbatim entry, including abbreviations and cross-references:
Evidence image filenames or stable scan links:
Lookup result: listed / explicitly marked vacant / no entry found /
               illegible / edition unavailable / address unresolved
Addresses, corner entries and cross-references checked:
Researcher and lookup date:
Interpretation or unresolved question, separate from transcription:
```

Use the actual edition date if a requested year is unavailable; do not label a 1961 observation as 1960. “No entry found” requires an actual page search and is different from a directory explicitly saying “vacant.” An alphabetical listing may be needed to identify the business type or resolve an address cross-reference; cite that page too.

A later business at the same address does not prove uninterrupted operation between observations. Three decade snapshots can miss a business that opened and closed between them. After these checks, inspect additional years around any change; 1930 and 1940 are also useful follow-ups for the gaps between the existing map editions. Do not assign an opening or closing year from a single listing or absence.

Once a record has a verified transcription and page evidence, a site entry can read: **“From the city directory — [actual edition year]: [business] listed at [printed address] ([directory title], [section], p. [page]).”** Until then, every 1960/1970/1980 field in this file remains pending.
