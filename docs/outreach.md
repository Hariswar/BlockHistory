# Partner outreach

The plan's last Phase 4 item is a named organization using or linking BlockHistory. Places
that fit, from the plan: St. Louis Public Library (Carondelet branch, and its local history
staff), the Carondelet Historical Society, St. Louis university geography or environmental
programs, and community gardens in Carondelet and Patch (soil testing is their everyday
question). Send one short email each; follow up once after two weeks.

## Draft email

> **Subject:** A free map of Carondelet's past land uses, 1908–1950
>
> Hello [name],
>
> I built BlockHistory (block-history.vercel.app), a free site that reads the Sanborn fire
> insurance maps for Carondelet and Patch from 1908, 1938 and 1950. Type an address and it
> lists past uses nearby that sometimes left soil contamination, like filling stations,
> foundries and dry cleaners, with the original map image for each one and the state's tank
> and cleanup records.
>
> I think it could help [your patrons researching a house / your members / gardeners deciding
> where to test soil]. Would you be willing to try it and tell me what's missing or confusing?
> If it's useful, I'd be grateful for a link from your [local history page / website].
>
> It's a personal project with no ads and no tracking cookies; the
> accuracy of each map year is published at block-history.vercel.app/accuracy.
>
> Thank you,
> [your name]

When one agrees, add their name and a one-line quote to the README and the About page.
